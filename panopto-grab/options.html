Panopto Grab

Watches network requests on Panopto pages for playlists and media files, reads each playlist to work out whether the recording is one file or many segments, and offers a download. Works without the session module.

Injects a small script into Panopto pages that reads the DeliveryInfo response, which network monitoring cannot see. Gives the session title, duration, slide timings, caption availability, and the direct podcast URL. Turning this off unregisters the injected scripts entirely.

Panopto sites on their own domain, such as panopto.example.edu, are outside the default permissions. Add one here.
